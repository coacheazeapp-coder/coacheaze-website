Questions

Martial Arts academy FAQ

Can CoachEaze track karate and taekwondo belts?

Yes. Award belt badges as students progress and rate techniques like kata, kicks and sparring.

Can I run kids and adult classes in different dojos?

Yes. Add each dojo as a centre and each class as a batch with its own instructor and timings.

Does it handle grading fees?

Yes. Charge monthly fees and one-off grading fees, with automatic reminders and receipts.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every feature is free for 22 days, with no payment needed. Then pick the plan that fits your academy size.

Do I need to install anything?

No. CoachEaze works in any browser at app.coacheaze.com, on phone, tablet or laptop. An Android app is coming soon.
