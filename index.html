Frequently Asked

Answers to the stuff academies actually ask

Does CoachEaze work for sports other than badminton?

Yes — CoachEaze supports badminton, tennis, cricket, football, basketball, swimming, table tennis, martial arts, volleyball and more. Each sport ships with its own standardised skill list that your coaches rate students on.

How does the AI face recognition attendance actually work?

Your coaches register every student's face once (a short photo capture with liveness verification). After that, the app recognises students via group photo ("Smart Capture") or a continuously scanning camera ("Auto-Detect") pointed at the doorway. All matching runs on the phone — no photos are uploaded.

Is student biometric data safe?

Face descriptors are encrypted at rest with AES-128 + HMAC. We're DPDP Act 2023 compliant by design — right-to-erasure, data export, consent logs and audit trail are built in. Biometric data never leaves your academy's tenant.

Can parents see their child's progress?

Yes. Parents log in with their own email and see their kid's attendance heatmap, fees, performance ratings, achievements and upcoming sessions — without cluttering the coach's inbox.

Do you integrate with UPI / payment gateways?

Payment gateway support (Razorpay, Cashfree, Stripe India) is on the Growth and Elite plans. Manual "Mark Paid" works everywhere from day one.

What happens if my internet goes down during class?

The attendance flow caches locally and syncs when you're back online. Face recognition runs entirely on-device so it's internet-optional.

Can I import my existing students?

Yes — Elite plans include onboarding assistance. We import your roster, batch structure and historical attendance from Excel/CSV.

How do I get a setup token?

Tap "Register your Academy" in the app and use the "Request a Token" button — or email hello@coacheaze.com. We respond within one business day.
