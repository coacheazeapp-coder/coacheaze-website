Questions

Badminton academy FAQ

Can I run multiple badminton courts and centres?

Yes. Add every centre and court-wise batch, assign coaches and set timings. Owners see everything in one dashboard.

Can coaches rate badminton skills like footwork and smash?

Yes. Coaches rate any skill from 1 to 5 stars with notes. Parents and players see the ratings and progress over time.

Does it handle monthly and quarterly badminton fees?

Yes. Set monthly, quarterly or annual fees and packages per batch. Reminders and receipts go out automatically.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every feature is free for 22 days, with no payment needed. Then pick the plan that fits your academy size.

Do I need to install anything?

No. CoachEaze works in any browser at app.coacheaze.com, on phone, tablet or laptop. An Android app is coming soon.
