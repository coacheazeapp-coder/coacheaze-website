Every sport

One app for every sports academy

CoachEaze is coaching and academy management software for every sport. If your coaches teach students in batches, CoachEaze runs the rest: face-recognition attendance, coach check-in, fees, salaries, skill ratings, homework and a live parent app.

🏸 Badminton academy management →

Run morning and evening batches across courts and centres. Coaches mark the whole batch with one photo, rate footwork, smashes and net play, and parents see progress after every session.

🏏 Cricket academy management →

Manage nets, age-group squads and summer camps. Track batting, bowling and fielding skills per player, collect monthly or term fees, and keep parents updated without a WhatsApp group full of reminders.

⚽ Football academy management →

From U-8 grassroots to U-17 squads: attendance in seconds, coach check-in at the ground, skill ratings for passing, dribbling and fitness, and a parent app that shows exactly how each child is doing.

🎾 Tennis academy management →

Court-wise batches, private lessons and group clinics in one place. Rate forehand, backhand and serve, schedule coaches across clubs and send fee receipts automatically.

🏓 Table Tennis academy management

Short, frequent sessions with many players per table. Mark attendance with a group photo, track skill growth and share homework drills with players between sessions.

🏊 Swimming academy management →

Pool batches by level and age, seasonal enrolments and strict timings. Coaches check in at the pool, attendance is marked from the deck and parents get updates on every new stroke learnt.

🏀 Basketball academy management

Teams, training batches and tournaments. Rate shooting, defence and conditioning, keep fees and salaries in order and give parents a clear view of attendance and progress.

🏑 Hockey academy management →

Turf batches, team squads and coach rosters across grounds. Coach check-in works only at the centre, so you always know who coached which session.

🏐 Volleyball academy management →

Group batches, team practice and fitness sessions. Track attendance, skills and fees for every player, and share announcements with the whole team in one tap.

🥋 Martial Arts, Karate & Taekwondo academy management →

Belt levels, grading days and dojo batches. Award badges as students progress, keep fee records clean and let parents follow every belt on their phone.

♟️ Chess academy management

Online and offline classes, rating-based groups and homework puzzles. Assign homework with deadlines, track progress and collect fees without chasing parents.

⛸️ Skating academy management

Rink batches for beginners to advanced skaters. Mark attendance quickly, rate balance and technique, and keep parents informed after every class.

🏃 Athletics & Running academy management

Track, field and fitness groups. Record timings and skill ratings, schedule coaches across grounds and share training plans with athletes.

🎯 Squash academy management

Court bookings, coaching batches and private lessons. Attendance, fees and skill tracking in one app for players, parents and coaches.

🤸 Gymnastics academy management

Age-group batches, skill levels and competition prep. Rate flexibility, strength and routines, and let parents see every milestone.

🥊 Boxing & Fitness academy management

Batches, personal training and memberships. Coach check-in, attendance and fee tracking keep a busy gym running smoothly.

🤼 Kabaddi & Wrestling academy management →

Squad training, fitness sessions and tournaments. Mark attendance, rate raids and tackles, and manage coach salaries in one place.

🧘 Yoga & Fitness Classes academy management

Morning and evening classes across studios. Simple attendance, membership fees and coach salaries, with no paperwork.

💃 Dance & Performing Arts academy management

Style-wise batches, recitals and term fees. The same tools work for any class where students learn in batches with a coach.
