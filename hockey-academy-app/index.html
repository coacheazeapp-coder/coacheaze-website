Hockey academy FAQ

Can I manage hockey turf slots and squads?

Yes. Each turf slot or squad becomes a batch with its own coach, timings and fees.

Can coaches rate hockey skills like drag flicks?

Yes. Add any skill, including stick work, passing and drag flicks, and rate it from 1 to 5 stars.

Can we send practice notices to parents?

Yes. Announcements reach the batch or the whole academy instantly in the app.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every feature is free for 22 days, with no payment needed. Then pick the plan that fits your academy size.

Do I need to install anything?

No. CoachEaze works in any browser at app.coacheaze.com, on phone, tablet or laptop. An Android app is coming soon.
