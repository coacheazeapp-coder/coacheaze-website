Questions

Swimming academy FAQ

Can I group swimmers by level and lane?

Yes. Create batches by level, age or lane, and move swimmers between batches as they progress.

Can parents track swimming strokes?

Yes. Coaches rate each stroke and parents see the progress and badges in the app.

Does CoachEaze handle summer swimming camps?

Yes. Create camp batches with their own fees and dates. Enrolment and reminders are handled for you.

Is there a free trial?

Yes. Every feature is free for 22 days, with no payment needed. Then pick the plan that fits your academy size.

Do I need to install anything?

No. CoachEaze works in any browser at app.coacheaze.com, on phone, tablet or laptop. An Android app is coming soon.
